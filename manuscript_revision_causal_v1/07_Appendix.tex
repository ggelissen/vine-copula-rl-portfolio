\subsection{Evidence Classes}

Three evidence classes are kept separate throughout the project. The frozen main evaluation is the original locked out-of-sample result. The no-vine, pretrained-only, ensemble-mechanism, and component analyses are post-holdout explanatory because they reuse its realised path. Retrospective walk-forward and external-panel studies can provide robustness and external-validity evidence, but only future non-overlapping windows frozen before access are eligible for a new confirmatory claim.

All variants use seeds 20261001--20261010. A missing or invalid seed invalidates the matched experiment rather than being replaced. Ensembles are constructed by averaging target weights and then recomputing drifted turnover, transaction costs, financing costs, and returns. The primary component family uses one-sided paired CRRA-utility effects with Holm adjustment across eight contrasts; algorithm comparisons are two-sided and exploratory. Circular moving-block bootstrap inference uses 9,999 replications and block length three.

\subsection{Minimum Replication Package}

A complete release should contain: (i) the raw adjusted-level panel or an immutable licensed-data reference and hash; (ii) the training marginal and neural-vine fits; (iii) synthetic and historical bundle manifests; (iv) all seed checkpoints, behaviour gates, schedules, code hashes, and data hashes; (v) frozen training and evaluation source snapshots; (vi) the main and explanatory archives with SHA-256 sidecars; (vii) failed operational-retry logs and an incident timeline; (viii) R, Python, package, CUDA, and container inventories; and (ix) a contents manifest covering every supplementary artifact. Manuscript tables and figures must be generated from those frozen artifacts rather than transcribed manually.
