# Frozen benchmark weight specification

The old functions in `eval/benchmarks.r` are useful prototypes, but their
wealth outputs are inadmissible for publication. Every replacement below must
emit the canonical weight-log schema and let the common evaluator compute all
realized returns and costs.

## Shared information and mandate

- Decision information ends on `decision_date`; no price, return, volatility,
  marginal state, copula state, or fitted parameter after that date is visible.
- Monthly weights sum to 1.0, gross exposure is at most 1.5, each long is at
  most 0.60, and each short is at least -0.20.
- The initial position is equal weight. Optimizers may account for the common
  turnover and financing rates ex ante, but realized costs are applied only by
  `publication_pipeline.py`.
- Hyperparameters, lookbacks, solver tolerances, simulation counts, and random
  seeds are frozen before the holdout batch. A solver failure is logged and
  fails the run; it does not fall back to a favorable portfolio.
- Each log contains exactly `window_id`, `decision_date`, `holding_end_date`,
  and one `w_<ASSET>` column per asset. It contains no realized return or
  self-reported wealth column.

## Minimum main-table strategies

1. **Equal weight.** Exactly 1/7 in every asset. Generated by the common
   evaluator, so it has no model-cache ambiguity.
2. **Constrained shrinkage mean--variance.** Expanding completed monthly
   history; positive-part James--Stein expected returns and closed-form OAS
   covariance shrinkage are re-estimated causally at every decision. CRRA
   gamma supplies the risk-aversion coefficient. Solve directly under the
   common bounds, net, gross, financing, and turnover terms.
3. **DCC-GARCH.** Fit using data available at the decision, forecast the next
   monthly covariance, and use the same expected-return convention as the
   mean--variance benchmark. Do not compare a daily one-step DCC simulation to
   a monthly RL return.
4. **Static vine.** Fit marginals and the vine once at the training cutoff;
   generate monthly scenarios with the same monthly marginal/serial machinery
   used by the corrected generator. Only portfolio optimization varies by
   date through current wealth/holdings if its objective requires them.
5. **Rolling vine.** Refit empirical monthly marginals, serial t copulas,
   D-vine order, and every static t edge only on the preregistered 60-month
   window available at each decision. This is a benchmark, not the proposed
   dependence model.
6. **Dynamic NN-vine optimizer without RL.** Use the same causal NN-vine
   snapshots and monthly scenarios as the proposed model, then solve a
   constrained CRRA/CVaR portfolio problem directly. This comparison is
   essential: it isolates whether RL adds value beyond the novel dependence
   model.
7. **No-vine LSTM-TD3.** Retrain ten preregistered seeds with the vine channel
   identically zero, while preserving its width, LSTM/TD3 parameter count,
   training data, projection, and optimisation budget.

The proposed model is the predeclared mean-weight ensemble of all 20
no-holdout-gate-passing full checkpoints. Individual checkpoints appear in the
seed-robustness appendix, not as 20 independent competitors in market tests.

## Required ablations

- no synthetic pre-training, with total update budget disclosed;
- no vine state;
- feed-forward encoder with matched parameter count;
- `lambda = 0` risk-neutral reward;
- unlevered long-only mandate (reported as a different mandate, not mixed into
  the common-constraint superiority claim);
- static instead of dynamic vine state;
- alternative/calibrated monthly scenario generator.

Every ablation is trained on multiple preregistered seeds and supplies weights
on the same locked realized panel. The artifact readers must remain fail-closed
until these runs exist.

## Benchmark acceptance tests before holdout execution

- perturb one future return and confirm every earlier weight is bitwise
  unchanged (causality test);
- shift or remove a decision date and confirm the common evaluator rejects the
  log;
- force an optimizer toward a corner and confirm long/short/net/gross
  constraints hold within `1e-6` without post-hoc clipping;
- rerun a seeded scenario method and confirm identical weights and input hashes;
- compare environment and common-evaluator net returns on a hand-calculated
  path to at least `1e-10`;
- inject a solver failure and confirm a non-zero exit with no fallback weights;
- verify every method uses the same asset order, calendar, cost rates, and
  initial equal-weight position.

## Inference and claims

The paired family contains (a) every strategy versus equal weight and (b) the
proposed ensemble versus every alternative. Holm adjustment covers the entire
declared family. A circular moving-block White-style reality check screens all
candidates jointly against equal weight. With only 23 complete monthly periods,
effect sizes and intervals dominate p-values; empirical 5% CVaR has only about
one or two tail observations and is descriptive.
